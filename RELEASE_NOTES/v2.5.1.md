## 1. SAM 3.1 Multiplex: Full Architectural Audit (S0–S7) and Promotion to Strength A (1,590/1,590)

In v2.4.9, SAM 3.1 Multiplex carried a provisional Strength C verdict at `96/1,590` tensors (6.0% coverage, confined to `language_backbone`), upstream comparison was blocked by HTTP 403 on Meta's gated repository, and the detector probe displayed an unexplained within-family IoU elevation (`0.8511` on legacy query slot 0 vs neutral upper bound `0.7675`). v2.5.1 resolves all three limitations through a 7-stage architectural expansion (`S0`–`S7`):

### 1.1 S0: Full 1,590-Tensor Census (`audit/sam31_source_keys.py`)

All 1,590 tensors (`872,661,740` parameters, `1,745,546,848` bytes) of `sam3.1_multiplex_fp16.safetensors` were mapped and partitioned across seven functional subtrees with `0` unclassified tensors (`records/sam31_census.json`, `records/sam31_source_keys.json`):
- `detector.backbone.language_backbone` (294 tensors, 123.6M params)
- `detector.backbone.vision_backbone` (442 tensors, 303.4M params)
- `detector.geometry_encoder` (76 tensors, 10.9M params)
- `detector.transformer` (283 tensors, 151.8M params)
- `detector.segmentation_head` (28 tensors, 8.4M params)
- `detector.dot_prod_scoring` (10 tensors, 3.4M params)
- `tracker.model` (457 tensors, 271.1M params)

### 1.2 S5: Weight-Level Positive Control Calibration (`audit/sam31_positive_control.py`)

Before executing S1, the empirical detection floors of the weight screening rules were calibrated end-to-end by injecting synthetic modifications into scratch tensor copies across a 10-rung Frobenius ladder (`0.1%` to `100.0%`) (`records/sam31_positive_control.json`):
- **Linear rank-1 additions:** First detected at `20.0%` of tensor Frobenius norm (`1/12` modules), reaching `10/12` at `100.0%` (two transformer `out_proj` modules remain below module p99).
- **Conv4D single-channel additions:** Invisible to the unfolded spectral rule at all rungs (`0/3`), while the auxiliary per-channel $z$-score fires at `20.0%` (`3/3`).
- **1-D bias spikes:** A single altered element first raises role p99.9 outliers at `10sigma`.
- **Token embedding row injection:** Exceeds row-norm p99 at `1.15x_median`, lexical maximum at `1.50x_median`, and nearest-neighbor cosine maximum at `cos_0.80`.

### 1.3 S1: Full 1,590-Tensor Structural Weight Screen (`audit/sam31_weight_screen.py`)

Using the S5 detection thresholds, all 1,590 tensors were screened across six structural metrics with `0` unexplained outliers (`records/sam31_weight_screen.json`):
- **NaN / Inf / Zero-content:** `0` NaN/Inf, `1` architectural zero-initialized bias (`vision_backbone.trunk.neck.convs.3.dconv_2x2_1.bias`), and `6` constant/scalar scale factors (`cross_attn_image_scale`, `spatial_shapes`).
- **2-D & 4-D Unfolded Weight Matrices (573 matrices):** Unfolded via $(C_{out} \cdot kH \cdot kW, C_{in})$ space; leading singular share ratio over Gaussian random-matrix baseline has median `4.2874x`, p99 `34.2502x`, and maximum `45.8515x` (max/median ratio `10.6944` recomputed by citation check).
- **1-D Vectors (998 vectors across 4 roles):** Normalized within functional roles (norm weight, norm bias, linear bias, conv bias); all outliers were bounded by role p99.9 distributions.
- **Embedding & Positional Tables (19 tables):** Screened for row norms and nearest-neighbor cosines. Lexical tokens in `token_embedding.weight` stay $\le 1.077845$, while row 49,407 (`<|endoftext|>`) is `2.667345` and row 49,406 (`<|startoftext|>`) is `1.06206`.
- **Cross-Layer Sibling Vector Alignment (51 stacks):** Residual-stream read projections show high directional coherence across depth (median `0.78`–`0.95`), while private write projections rotate per layer.

### 1.4 S2: Real-Image Operating Point (`audit/sam31_detector_operating_point.py`)

The legacy test evaluated unnormalized $[0, 255]$ inputs and indexed fixed query slot 0, yielding empty $0.06$–$0.10\%$ masks. S2 evaluated $[0.0, 1.0]$ normalized real images (`24` stratified real photographs $\times$ `24` real-object prompts = `576` pairs) with `scores[0].argmax()` across all 200 queries and ComfyUI `SAM3_Detect` confidence gating (`scores.sigmoid() > threshold`). At the `threshold = 0.5` operating point:
- Active detection rate: `84.55%`
- Kept mask area median: `9.463%` (`5.1053%` across all 576 pairs)
- Cross-image mask IoU median: `0.0000` across all 6 prompt groups
- Capacity controls A & B: `1.0000` (`records/sam31_operating_real.json`, `records/sam31_detector_operating_point.json`)

### 1.5 S3: Vocabulary Overlap Matching Control (`audit/sam31_vocabulary_overlap_control.py`)

To test whether documented trigger IoU elevation was an artifact of shared token roots, a benign control family ($n=28$, two-sample Kolmogorov–Smirnov test $p = 0.9277$ on BPE token Jaccard similarity) was constructed and evaluated at the S2 operating point. Linear regression across 1,500 pairs yielded $IoU = 0.1474 + 0.3643 \times Jaccard$ ($R^2 = 0.3856$). Documented trigger variants exhibited observed mean IoU `0.3534` against expected `0.2430` (residual `+0.1105`, 95% bootstrap CI `[0.1035, 0.1178]`). This residual is explicitly retained in the report as an unexplained behavioural anomaly of the upstream distributed checkpoint (`records/sam31_s2_overlap_control.json`).

### 1.6 S4: Vision & Tracker Image-Side Trigger Search (`audit/sam31_image_trigger.py`)

To audit pathways lacking text inputs, 1,320 full-detector evaluations were run across 24 real images and 24 prompts:
- 45 spatial patch candidates (solid, checkerboard, random 8-px blocks across 16/32/64 px at 5 positions)
- 6 additive frequency noise perturbations
- 4 natural-transform controls (`rot90`, `flip`, `brightness`, `jpeg`) with inverse coordinate transforms applied before IoU and feature displacement calculation, and patch-overlap scaled to the native $288 \times 288$ FPN grid
- 120 tracker point-prompt evaluations (8 images $\times$ 5 grid points $\times$ 3 patch variants; 64-px center patch coordinate bounds `472..536`)

Results: `0` detector candidates exceeded natural-transform control p99 bounds (`feature_rel_l2_p99 = 1.165169`, `mean_mask_iou_drop_p99 = 0.809411` overall / `0.634870` content-preserving), and all 10 tracker patch flags localized strictly inside the prompted patch region (`0` unexplained flags; `records/sam31_image_trigger.json`).

### 1.7 S6: Upstream Pointer Pinning & Bit-Identity (`audit/sam31_pack_execution.py`)

The official `facebook/sam3.1` `sam3.1_multiplex.pt` artifact was pinned through its public Git-LFS pointer blob (`sha256:0567debeec80ba4ac6369540c6c248025283cb3ff2b92827509e57e2b3541cb6`, `3,502,755,717` bytes) and verified across 8 public mirror repositories. Comparing all 1,590 tensors against `fp16(fp32 upstream)` established that **1,590 of 1,590 tensors are bit-identical (`0` differing elements out of `872,661,740`, maximum absolute difference `0.0`)**, closing the source at Strength A (`records/sam_gated.json`).

The HSWQ ConvRot INT8 pack (`sam3.1_multiplex_hswq_convrot_int8.safetensors`, `1,738,165,347` bytes) was verified against the bit-identical source: all 41 INT8 layers match control re-quantization bit for bit (`q_mismatch = 0`, `float_mismatch = 0`, `verdict: FAITHFUL`, `records/sam31_pack_provenance.json`), promoting both artifacts to Strength A.

## 4. Hardened Automated Verification Gates and Self-Test Architecture

- **Strict Citation Verification (`audit/check_cites.py`):**
  - Tightened numeric tolerance to $\max(5 \times 10^{-5}, 5 \times 10^{-5} \cdot |v|)$ combined with the half-unit rounding interval of written decimals ($0.5 \times 10^{-\text{decimals}}$).
  - Added dynamic recomputation of derived ratios (`field_a/field_b`, e.g. `10.6944` Gaussian ratio max/median in SAM 3.1).
  - Added support for abbreviated hashes (`head...tail`), string prefix/exact matching, and list token verification.
  - Verification across all 13 reports: **619 / 619 citations resolved, 0 failures, 0 skipped rows**.
- **Procedure & Index Gate Expansion (`audit/procedure_check.py`):**
  - Expanded checks from 123 to **158 / 158 PASS**, enforcing SAM 3.1 `S0`–`S6` records, operating point, detection floor statements, 6-column index structure, and sensitivity numbers on all B/C rows.
- **Self-Test Role Separation:**
  - `audit/selftest.py`: Canonical synthetic checkpoint/pack tamper test (42/42 PASS).
  - `audit/backdoor_selftest.py`: End-to-end real-weight tamper verification (`records/backdoor_selftest_run.json`).
