## Overview

v2.4.8 introduces complete in-workflow **HSWQ V1.1 Trajectory-Impact Hybrid Quantization** support for **Stable Diffusion 1.5 (SD1.5)** directly inside ComfyUI. 

Following the CLI trajectory-impact release in v2.4.7, this release integrates the entire three-step pipeline—from single-layer trajectory divergence measurement to hybrid pack generation and deterministic 25-seed latent trajectory acceptance verification—into native ComfyUI nodes, eliminating all manual terminal execution.

---

## 1. ComfyUI Node Architecture & Pipeline

The SD1.5 in-workflow quantization pipeline consists of three dedicated nodes designed for deterministic execution, reproducibility, and zero-cost parameter experimentation:

### 1.1. Step 1: Trajectory Impact Measurement (`HSWQ Diag Impact (ConvRot INT8)`)
- **Category:** `HSWQ/Quantize`
- **Architecture Option:** `model_type = "SD1.5"`
- **Mechanism:** Runs an in-process, per-layer trajectory divergence measurement across all 282 ConvRot-eligible UNet layers (25 steps, fixed prompt, CFG 7.0, seed 42).
- **Perturbation Frame:** Replaces each layer's weight one at a time with its ConvRot INT8 reconstruction in the **original frame** (`(q * s) @ H`), holding all other layers at pristine FP16. This avoids measuring the rotation itself and accurately identifies downstream error propagation.
- **Output:** Writes the comprehensive trajectory sensitivity profile to `impact/impact_sd15_traj_<model_stem>.json`.

### 1.2. Zero-Cost Profile Reuse (`HSWQ Load Diag Impact Json`)
- **Category:** `HSWQ/Quantize`
- **Mechanism:** Dynamically populates stored profile JSONs (`impact_sd15_traj_*.json` under `<repo>/impact/`) in a dropdown.
- **Workflow Integration:** Connects alongside `HSWQ Diag Impact` to an `ImpactSwitch` (`Switch (Any)`):
  - `select = 1`: Runs a fresh Step 1 measurement.
  - `select = 2`: Reuses an existing impact JSON instantly.
- Enables sweeping protection parameter $K$ (e.g. $K=80$ vs $K=82$) and pack configurations without repeating the 12-minute Step 1 profiling run.

### 1.3. Step 2 & Step 3: Hybrid Pack Builder & Acceptance Gate (`HSWQ Quantize ConvRot INT8`)
- **Category:** `HSWQ/Quantize`
- **Architecture Option:** `model_type = "SD1.5"`
- **Input Connections:** Consumes `model`, `clip`, `vae`, and `impact_json` from the `ImpactSwitch`.
- **Step 2 Hybrid Pack Building:**
  - **Top-$K$ FP16 Protection:** Retains the $K$ highest-impact layers (recommended $K=80$ to $82$) in pristine FP16 with weights byte-identical to base.
  - **ConvRot INT8 Quantization:** Converts all remaining 2D `Linear` and 4D `Conv2d` layers to FULL ConvRot INT8 (Hadamard group size 256, FP32 `weight_scale`, `.comfy_quant` metadata).
  - **Systematic Bias Correction:** Opt-in INT8 bias folding (`E[(W_q - W) x] ~ (W_q - W) @ E[x]`) using activation means collected from calibration prompts, cancelling systematic drift on quantized layers.
  - **CLIP Text Encoder Pass-Through:** Preserves `cond_stage_model.*` completely untouched at original FP16 (zero `.comfy_quant` or `.weight_scale` artifacts).
  - **Embedded VAE Removal:** Strips `first_stage_model.*` from the output pack, saving storage (~1.16 GB HSWQ pack vs 2.03 GB base).
- **Step 3 Deterministic Trajectory Gate:**
  - Executes immediate 25-seed deterministic trajectory comparison (512x512, 25 steps, CFG 7.0) against the FP16 base.
  - Acceptance threshold: mean final cosine $\ge 0.95$ and **0/25 bifurcated**.
  - Emits live logging and final score summaries directly to connected `ShowText` nodes.

### Key Benchmark Findings:
1. **Zero Bifurcation Guarantee:** Across all 175 seeds evaluated on 7 diverse checkpoints, neither HSWQ nor Native produces a single bifurcated seed (0/175, 0.0%).
2. **Latent Fidelity Gain:** HSWQ achieves a family mean final cosine of **0.96914** vs **0.95266** for Native (+0.01648), outperforming Native on 6 of 7 models.
3. **Error Suppression:** Latent MSE is reduced from **1.6656** down to **1.0521** (~37% overall error reduction across the family).
4. **Catastrophic Drift Prevention:** On complex multi-model merges (`0.7_0.7hassaku+0.3dreamshaper_8_0.3anylora`), Native quantization collapses on seed 42 down to cosine 0.63851 (max step drop 0.0469, borderline bifurcation). HSWQ anchors the trajectory securely at 0.88097, preserving output coherence.
