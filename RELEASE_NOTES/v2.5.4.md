## Overview

v2.5.4 quantizes and audits the SD1.5 ControlNet series: fourteen SD1.5 ControlNet packs — tile, canny, depth, ip2p, shuffle, inpaint, lineart, m-lsd, normalbae, openpose, scribble, seg, softedge and the s2 lineart-anime pack — join the SDXL InstantID pack already published, and every one of the fifteen carries the same four measurements: a protection set measured on its own checkpoint by a full trajectory sweep, a weight-level closure against the publisher's release, a runtime route that applies every declared layer through the INT8 kernel, and a backdoor verdict of *no backdoor detected* at strength A over the file's whole tensor population. The verdict index now holds twenty-eight packs with its two-axis partition intact, and the gate run for this release reports all five gates PASS.

## 2. The protection set belongs to the checkpoint it was measured on

Each SD1.5 pack was swept over its **142** eligible modules (20 steps, seed 42, cfg 7.0) and the InstantID pack over its **400**; a layer is kept in FP16 when its own drift exceeds 0.15. The measured spread is wide, and it is the reason the set is measured per checkpoint instead of copied: the flattest ranking in the series is openpose, where only two layers cross the threshold (0.176361 and 0.159972), while ip2p and softedge each put forty-two above it, and on softedge the whole ranking is dominated by one module.

| tile | `input_hint_block.10` | 0.478440 |
| canny | `input_blocks.1.1.proj_out` | 0.782379 |

| openpose | `input_blocks.1.0.in_layers.2` | 0.176361 |

The softedge figure deserves its own sentence: `input_hint_block.2` measures **46.853904**, two orders of magnitude above the next module in that graph (0.686831), so the threshold cut below it is a choice rather than a natural gap — which the pack's record and verdict both state. The drift list and the FP16-kept count are different populations: the sweep walks all 142 modules of the graph, convolutions included, while the kept count is over the runtime's 53 Linear layers - so on ip2p 42 Linear layers cross the threshold and all 42 are kept, and on softedge 42 modules cross it while 29 Linear layers are kept in FP16.
